### A roadmap tailored to your background

    Given your interests in **Java/Spring Boot, microservices, enterprise architecture, RAG, and AI-assisted SDLC**, I'd suggest this sequence over the next 6–9 months:
    
    1. Machine Learning Specialization (Coursera)
    2. DeepLearning.AI's Generative AI and Prompt Engineering short courses
    3. LangChain Academy + LlamaIndex tutorials
    4. Build an enterprise RAG application using Spring Boot AI or FastAPI
    5. Earn either **Azure AI-102** or **AWS AI Practitioner** (depending on your organization's cloud)
    6. Complete a capstone project such as an **AI-assisted SDLC platform** with agents, RAG, code review, documentation generation, and CI/CD integration.
    
    This combination will generally make you more competitive for roles such as AI Engineer, GenAI Engineer, AI Solutions Architect, or Enterprise AI Architect than simply accumulating many certificates.
